Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 12, 2025

Pizza Hut is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to achieve this goal.

Our Commitment

We strive to make our website and services accessible to everyone, including individuals with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities. Our goal is to provide a seamless and inclusive experience for all users.

Accessibility Standards

We aim to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, which provide guidance on making digital content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities.

Features That Support Accessibility

  • Semantic HTML5 structure for screen readers
  • Keyboard navigable interfaces and forms
  • High contrast color schemes and readable fonts
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • ARIA roles and labels for dynamic content and controls
  • Responsive design for use with various devices and assistive technologies

Ongoing Improvements

Our team regularly audits and tests our website using automated tools and manual checks to identify and address any accessibility issues. We welcome feedback from users to help us enhance the accessibility of our services.
